Part 2 Cue Card (2 minutes)

Describe a subject you studied at school or university that you found particularly interesting.

You should say:

  • What the subject was and why you chose it
  • What the teacher or professor was like
  • What specific activities or research you did
  • And explain how this subject has influenced your career or thinking.

💡 Try to use at least 5 of these words: archaeology, astronomy, economics, geology, linguistics, psychology, sociology, analyse, claim, define, evaluate.
